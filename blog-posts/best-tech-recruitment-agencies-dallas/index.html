The 5 best tech recruitment agencies in Dallas ranked for 2026. Compare fees, timelines & specializations across telecom, automotive tech & more.

2. Motion Recruitment

Motion Recruitment is a technology-focused staffing firm with a Dallas office that genuinely specializes in tech — they don't place accountants and call themselves a tech agency. Their DFW desk has placed engineers at Texas Instruments and several telecom contractors in the AT&T ecosystem.

  • Best for: Mid-market companies needing contract or contract-to-hire engineers quickly
  • Specializes in: Software development, cybersecurity, data engineering; strong in contract staffing
  • Fee structure: Contract markup (typically 40–55% on hourly rate); direct-hire fees around 18–22% of first-year salary
  • Typical timeline: 2–3 weeks for contract roles; 4–6 weeks for direct hire
  • Company size: Best suited to mid-market; strong bench for contract talent

3. Apex Systems

One of the largest IT staffing firms in the US, Apex has a significant Dallas presence built largely around enterprise clients and government-adjacent tech contracts. Their volume is high; their personalization is lower.

  • Best for: Large enterprises needing multiple contract hires simultaneously, especially for legacy system maintenance or IT ops roles
  • Specializes in: IT infrastructure, enterprise application support, staff augmentation at scale
  • Fee structure: Contract markup 45–60%; direct-hire fees 18–25% of base salary
  • Typical timeline: 1–2 weeks for contract; 5–8 weeks for direct hire at senior levels
  • Company size: Enterprise and large mid-market; less tailored for startups or niche technical roles

4. Insight Global

Insight Global operates a large DFW office and is well-known among Dallas hiring managers for high-volume staffing across IT and light industrial. Their tech desk covers the basics well but can struggle with highly specialized engineering roles in verticals like embedded systems (critical for Texas Instruments suppliers) or automotive OTA software.

  • Best for: Companies with ongoing, volume staffing needs across generalist IT roles
  • Specializes in: Help desk, IT support, project management, business analysis; broader tech generalism
  • Fee structure: Contract markup 40–55%; direct-hire fees approximately 18–22%
  • Typical timeline: Fast for contract (1–2 weeks); 4–7 weeks for permanent placement
  • Company size: Mid-market to enterprise; particularly strong for Southwest Airlines-type IT ops teams

5. Robert Half Technology

Robert Half Technology (now operating under Talent Solutions) is the most recognizable name in Dallas tech staffing, with decades of market presence. Brand recognition helps attract passive candidates, but their generalist model means technical depth can be inconsistent depending on which individual recruiter you're assigned.

  • Best for: Companies that need a known brand for internal stakeholder buy-in, or that need finance-adjacent tech roles (fintech, ERP)
  • Specializes in: ERP/SAP, IT management, finance systems, broad software development
  • Fee structure: Direct-hire fees typically 20–25% of first-year salary; some retained search options
  • Typical timeline: 4–8 weeks on average for permanent roles
  • Company size: Works across all sizes but most efficient for established companies with structured HR processes

How to Choose the Right Tech Recruitment Agency in Dallas

Dallas's tech market has three distinct hiring contexts that should guide your agency choice: the enterprise corridor (AT&T, Toyota, Southwest Airlines Tech), the semiconductor and hardware ecosystem (Texas Instruments and its supply chain), and the consumer/product startup layer (Match Group, emerging DFW SaaS companies). Each demands a different recruiter profile.

  • Role seniority: For Staff+ engineers or director-level PMs, use a boutique or specialized agency — volume shops like Apex and Insight Global rarely have the networks for passive senior talent
  • Engagement type: Contract or staff augmentation? Motion Recruitment and Apex outperform boutiques on volume. Permanent, high-quality hire? Hypertalent or Motion for tech-specific roles.
  • Technical specificity: Embedded systems, automotive OTA, or telecom protocol engineers require recruiters who can actually screen for those skills — ask agencies how they technically assess candidates before you commit
  • Fee risk tolerance: If cash flow matters, a no-retainer success-based model (Hypertalent) eliminates downside risk entirely
  • Timeline urgency: Need someone in under 3 weeks? Only agencies with pre-vetted pipelines will reliably hit that window
  • Dallas salary calibration: No state income tax means a $160K Dallas offer competes with a $175–180K offer in California after taxes — your agency should be making this argument to candidates on your behalf

Agency vs DIY Hiring in Dallas: When Each Makes Sense

DIY hiring via LinkedIn Recruiter or job boards works well for junior roles with high applicant volume. For senior or specialized roles in Dallas's competitive market — where Toyota Plano and AT&T are fishing in the same talent pool as your startup — the math often favors agency placement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do tech recruitment agencies in Dallas typically charge?

Direct-hire fees in Dallas generally run 18–25% of first-year base salary for permanent placements. Contract staffing markups typically range 40–60% on top of the hourly bill rate. Success-based agencies like Hypertalent charge zero upfront — fees are only due when a hire is made.

How long does it take to fill a senior engineering role in Dallas?

With a specialized agency using pre-vetted pipelines, expect your first shortlist within one week and a closed placement in 2–4 weeks. Generalist agencies or DIY hiring typically runs 6–12 weeks for senior roles. Dallas's no-state-income-tax advantage can accelerate candidate decisions when your recruiter is actively making that case.

Do Dallas tech recruitment agencies offer placement guarantees?

Most reputable agencies offer a replacement guarantee of 30–90 days — if the placed candidate leaves within that window, they'll replace them at no additional fee. Always confirm the guarantee period and conditions in writing before signing.

Which Dallas industries are hardest to hire for right now?

As of 2026, the tightest talent markets in DFW are: telecom software engineering (AT&T's demand is enormous relative to local supply), automotive embedded and OTA software (Toyota Plano competes globally for this talent), and consumer mobile product engineers (Match Group has raised compensation expectations for that niche significantly).

Can a Dallas recruitment agency help hire for remote or hybrid roles?

Yes — and this is increasingly relevant. Many DFW employers are using Dallas's cost-of-living advantage to attract engineers who would otherwise target NYC or SF. Agencies with national or global reach (like Hypertalent, which operates across US, Switzerland, and Singapore) can source candidates willing to relocate or work hybrid from the DFW area.
