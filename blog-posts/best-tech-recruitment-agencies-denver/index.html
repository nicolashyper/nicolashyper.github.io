The result: a senior full-stack or cloud engineer in Denver receives an average of 4.2 inbound recruiter messages per week, according to LinkedIn Talent Insights data from Q1 2026. What to Ask Any Denver Tech Recruiter Before You Engage

The difference between a great hire and a six-month mistake often comes down to the questions you ask your recruiter upfront. Before signing any agreement with a tech recruitment agency in Denver, get specific answers to the following:

  1. What is your technical vetting process? Agencies that can't describe a structured technical screen—beyond a phone screen—are not actually vetting candidates.
  2. How many senior engineers have you placed in [your specific stack] in the last 12 months? Demand evidence, not anecdote.
  3. What is your offer acceptance rate? Best-in-class is above 80%. Below 60% means candidates are being over-presented to multiple clients simultaneously.
  4. Do you work retained, contingency, or hybrid? Retained and hybrid models align incentives; pure contingency creates pressure to submit fast, not submit right.
  5. What is your replacement guarantee? Twelve months is the industry-leading standard. Anything under 90 days is a red flag.

Total compensation packages—including equity and bonuses—typically add 20–35% on top of base for growth-stage and late-stage companies. Any recruiter quoting materially lower figures is either working with outdated data or targeting under-market candidates, which is a sign of low sourcing quality.
