FAQ

Questions founders ask

Something else? Email us and we'll answer the same day.

What roles do you hire for?

Mostly technical and early-team roles at venture-backed startups: software, AI and forward-deployed engineers, mechanical, electrical and robotics engineers, product and design, and go-to-market hires like founding AEs and solutions engineers. We also run specialist searches in finance, legal and crypto.

How fast will we see candidates?

Usually within the first week. The average search goes from kickoff to signed offer in about two weeks, depending on how specialised the role is.

How is this different from getting a stack of resumes?

Everyone we send you has already seen the role, the company and the compensation, and told us they want the conversation. You spend your time on calls, not on chasing people who were never interested.

Where do you recruit?

Across the United States and Europe, for on-site, hybrid and remote teams. We also run relocation searches when the best people live somewhere else.

Can you work alongside our in-house team or ATS?

Yes. We can plug into your ATS and interview process, or run the whole search for you, from the brief to the offer.

What does it cost?

It depends on the role and the scope of the search. We'll walk you through it on the intro call. There's no commitment to book that call.
