Got an email from us? Here's how to check.

HyperTalent is a tech recruiting agency with offices in New York and Dubai. We hire engineers, hardware builders, product and GTM people for venture-backed startups. Our recruiters email from several domains to keep delivery reliable. They are all listed below.

Domains we send from

An email from an address at any of these domains is from the HyperTalent team.

@hypertalent.me@hyper-talent.com@recruitenclave.org@gothypertalent.com@workhypertalent.com@hqhirehypertalent.com@hqhypertalent.com@hrhypertalent.com@apphypertalent.com@teamhypertalent.com@globalhypertalent.com@hypertalent-agency.com@agency-hypertalent.com@nimblepathways.com

Older threads may also come from @hirehypertalent.com, @gethypertalent.com, @hypertalentcareers.com, @careershypertalent.com.

What we never ask for

We never charge candidates. We never ask for payment, bank details, passwords or ID documents, and we never ask you to install anything. The hiring company pays us.

Why the first email is vague

Our clients' searches are confidential, so the first message describes the role without naming the company. Reply and we'll tell you the company and send the full job description.

How we found you

We match public professional profiles to open roles. Reply "not interested" and we stop. Email us and we'll delete what we hold about you.

Still unsure?

Write to nicolas@hypertalent.me from your own email, or reach us on LinkedIn.
